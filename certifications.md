# Certifications

Short version: **the portfolio from this course matters more than any cert.** A cert gets your resume past a filter. The repo and your quality report get you the offer.

Checked October 2026. Exam details change, so confirm on the official page before you book.

| Cert | What it proves | When | Prep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1](https://www.istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf) (Foundation) | Core testing vocabulary. Required before the specialist certs | Skip if you already hold it | [ISTQB Foundation Level training](https://www.udemy.com/course/foundation-level-training/) (Tarek Roshdy), 4.7 from about 16,700 ratings |
| [ISTQB CT-GenAI](https://istqb.org/certifications/gen-ai/) (Testing with Generative AI) | You can use GenAI in testing and understand its risks. Approved July 2025, syllabus v1.1 April 2026 | After week 4 | Official syllabus and sample exam (free), plus [CT-GenAI prep](https://www.udemy.com/course/istqb-certified-tester-in-ai-testing-gen-ai-preparation/), 4.4 from about 420 ratings |
| [ISTQB CT-AI v2.0](https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-ai-testing-ct-ai/) (AI Testing) | You can test AI systems themselves, including ML models and LLMs | After you land the role (optional) | [CT-AI practice tests](https://www.udemy.com/course/istqb-certified-tester-ai-testing-ct-ai-practice-exams) |

## Which one first?

1. Hold CTFL already? Go straight to **CT-GenAI**. QA hiring managers know the ISTQB name, so it reads well on a resume
2. Your company runs on one cloud? A cloud AI fundamentals cert for that cloud helps you talk to platform teams. It's a nice-to-have, not a must

Budget roughly CA$350 to CA$450 per ISTQB exam (approximate, it varies by exam provider and country; verify before booking).
